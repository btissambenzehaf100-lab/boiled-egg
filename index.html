Boiled Egg Recipe

A simple guide to making perfectly boiled eggs

Ingredients Steps Nutrition facts

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients you will need:

  • Eggs
  • Water
  • Salt

Steps

Follow these steps to make boiled eggs:

  1. Place the eggs in a saucepan.
  2. Cover the eggs with cold water.
  3. Bring the water to a boil.
  4. Reduce the heat and simmer until cooked.
  5. Transfer the eggs to cold water.
  6. Peel and serve.
